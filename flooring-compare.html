Quick Guide

A simple starting point based on the construction of each flooring type.

Choose engineered hardwood for

A real-wood surface, natural variation and wider premium plank options.

Choose WPC for

Waterproof performance with a thicker core and a softer, quieter feel underfoot.

Choose SPC for

Waterproof rigid-core performance in active residential or light-commercial spaces.
